# Enterprise Sales Operations Policy & Discount Guidelines (FY 2025)

## Overview
This document specifies standard commercial terms, pricing guidelines, and discount authority levels for enterprise accounts.

## Product Hierarchy and Base Pricing
1. **Aurora Laptop:** Electronics flagship, MSRP $1,200.
2. **Nimbus Phone:** Electronics tier 1, MSRP $800.
3. **Pulse Tablet:** Electronics tier 2, MSRP $450.
4. **Orbit Smartwatch:** Wearables, MSRP $250.
5. **Zephyr Headphones:** Accessories, MSRP $150.
6. **Echo Speaker:** Accessories, MSRP $120.

## Approval Workflows for External Communications
- Any customer communication containing contract amendments or pricing concessions must undergo formal executive review before transmission.
- Outbound marketing emails and automated Slack channel notifications dispatched via workflow automation require human operator approval.
