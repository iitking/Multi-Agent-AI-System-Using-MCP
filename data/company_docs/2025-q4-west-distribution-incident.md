# West Region Distribution Center Outage Report (Q3/Q4 2025)

## Executive Summary
From September 1 to November 25, 2025, the primary West Region fulfillment center in Reno experienced severe operational disruptions. An automated sorting and conveyor system failure, followed by unexpected warehouse flooding, severely restricted outgoing logistics.

## Timeline and Incident Details
- **September 2025:** Facility operated at 40% dispatch capacity due to conveyor breakdown. Outgoing shipping backlogs accumulated.
- **October 2025:** Secondary distribution routing mitigated backlogs partially, but regional fulfillment times surged from 2 days to 14 days. Customer order cancellations increased.
- **November 2025:** Repairs completed on November 20; testing concluded November 26.
- **December 2025:** Facility returned to 100% throughput capacity in time for holiday shipping.

## Financial and Commercial Impact
- Regional shipping volume dropped approximately 55% across all product lines during September, October, and November 2025.
- Estimated monthly revenue loss in the West region exceeded $300,000 per month compared to baseline.
- Demand rebounded fully in December 2025 once delivery timelines normalized.
