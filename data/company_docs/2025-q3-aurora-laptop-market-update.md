# Product Strategy Memo: Aurora Laptop Performance & Competitive Pressure

## Background
The Aurora Laptop ($1,200 list price) represents the flagship device in our Electronics category. Through August 2025, Aurora Laptop benefited from an introductory 10% promotional campaign across all channels.

## Key Market Developments in Q3/Q4 2025
- **End of Promotional Campaign:** The 10% promotional discount concluded on August 31, 2025, restoring list price to $1,200.
- **Competitor Launch:** In early September 2025, competitor Apex Technologies released their 'Zenith 14' model at $999 with significant marketing spend.
- **Sales Volume Impact:** Aurora Laptop unit sales contracted by approximately 30% across North, South, and East regions during September, October, and November 2025 due to the price reset and competitor pressure.
- **December Recovery:** Holiday bundle campaigns and seasonal corporate year-end procurement restored volume in December 2025.

## Strategic Recommendations
- Evaluate mid-tier bundle configurations for Aurora Laptop to protect against sub-$1,000 rival entries.
- Maintain regional inventory buffers to prevent fulfillment constraints.
